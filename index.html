Research profile

Dhan's work focuses on solid-state spin qubits, nitrogen-vacancy (NV) center-based magnetometry, quantum sensing platforms, and materials/device integration. Current work includes scanning NV microscopy, cryogenic NV sensing, magnetic materials, and spin dynamics.

02 / Research

Current research directions

Experimental platform

High-sensitivity NV sensing system

Thin-film fabrication and characterization combined with a custom single-NV scanning microscopy platform, microwave engineering, electromagnet bias fields, magnetic-field control, thermal and electrical fields control, and LabVIEW automation.

Cryogenic magnetism

2D magnets & single-spin sensing

Cryogenic NV measurements from 2–300 K using microwave delivery structures and DC/AC sensing protocols including ODMR, Rabi, Ramsey, Spin-echo, T1, T2, XY8, and CPMG.

Magnetic materials

Magnetic domains & spin dynamics

Nanoscale stray-field mapping, quantitative magnetic-domain analysis, image reconstruction, coherence measurements, and magnetic-noise spectroscopy for quantum materials. Manipulation and control of domain walls, skyrmions, and spin canting.
